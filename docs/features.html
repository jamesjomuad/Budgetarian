Features

Everything you need to manage your money

Budgetarian gives you simple, powerful tools for everyday money management — all on your device.

Budgets & Folders

Organize your finances into budgets that represent time periods — a month, a quarter, a project. Group budgets into folders for higher-level organization by year, purpose, or any category you choose.

  • Create budgets with start and end dates
  • Group budgets into customizable folders
  • Period shortcuts for last, this, and next month
  • Drag-to-reorder folders and budgets
  • Swipe to archive budgets with undo

Expense & Income Tracking

Record every transaction with amount, date, tag, and notes. Track paid and unpaid expenses to stay on top of bills and obligations.

  • Record income and expenses with tags
  • Track paid and unpaid payment status
  • Optional time and notes per transaction
  • Clone transactions for quick reuse
  • Move transactions between budgets

Transaction Breakdowns

Split transactions into detailed breakdown items, each with their own dates, times, and amounts. Perfect for splitting a grocery bill or tracking installment payments.

  • Add multiple breakdown items per transaction
  • Each item has its own date, time, and notes
  • Reorder items with drag and drop
  • Multi-select for batch operations
  • Clone breakdown items for recurring entries

Calendar & Reminders

View all your transactions in a monthly calendar. Set reminders for upcoming payments and recurring expenses so you never miss an obligation.

  • Monthly calendar view with daily timeline
  • Quick transaction editing from calendar
  • Set reminders with custom date and time
  • Standalone reminders for non-transaction events
  • Local push notifications on Android

Receipts & OCR

Attach receipt photos to transactions and let Budgetarian automatically recognize text from the image. Review the detected text before adding it to your records.

  • Multiple image attachments per transaction
  • Camera capture directly from the app
  • On-device text recognition (OCR)
  • Review and edit recognized text
  • Persistent receipt images

Dashboard & Insights

Get a clear picture of your finances at a glance. The home dashboard shows income, expenses, and remaining balances across all your budgets and folders.

  • Home insights dashboard
  • Per-tag spending breakdown
  • Paid, unpaid, and expected amount separation
  • Search across folders and transactions
  • Dedicated Budgets tab for overview

Import & Export

Export individual budgets to JSON, Excel, CSV, invoice, or report formats. Import budgets from files with a preview before committing. Full backup and restore keeps everything safe.

  • Export budgets to JSON, Excel, CSV, and more
  • Import budgets with preview and folder selection
  • Full backup export and import
  • Merge or restore options on import
  • Save backups to an Android folder
