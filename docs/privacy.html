Privacy

Your finances stay yours.

Budgetarian is built with a local-first philosophy. Your financial data never leaves your device.

Local-First Data Storage

All your financial data — budgets, transactions, tags, settings, and receipts — is stored locally on your device using SQLite. Nothing is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

No Account Required

Budgetarian does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. You can start using the app immediately after installation.

What Data Stays on Your Device

The following data is stored locally and never transmitted:

  • Budgets, folders, and their associated metadata
  • Transactions, breakdown items, and their amounts
  • Tags and tag library
  • Receipt images and recognized text
  • Reminders and notification schedules
  • App settings (display currency, theme preferences)
  • Search history and user preferences

Backup & Export

Budgetarian allows you to export your data for backup purposes:

  • Full backup export: Creates a JSON file containing all your budgets, transactions, tags, and settings. You choose where to save this file.
  • Budget export: Export individual budgets to JSON, Excel, CSV, invoice, or report formats.
  • Backup import: Import a previously exported backup file to restore or merge your data.

All export and import operations are initiated by you and stored in locations you choose. Budgetarian does not automatically upload or sync your data to any external service.

Permissions

Budgetarian requests the following Android permissions:

  • Camera: Used to photograph receipts for transaction attachments.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver reminder notifications for upcoming payments and recurring expenses.
  • Storage access: Used when you choose to save backups or import files from your device.

These permissions are used only for the stated purposes. Budgetarian does not request access to your contacts, location, phone state, or other sensitive data.

Third-Party Services

Budgetarian does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or cloud services. There are no SDKs that collect usage data or personal information.

Note: This privacy policy describes the current implementation of Budgetarian. If the app's data practices change in the future, this page will be updated accordingly.

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, uninstalling the app will remove all your financial data. You can also manually delete individual budgets, transactions, or tags from within the app at any time.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or Budgetarian's data practices, please visit the GitHub repository or open an issue.
