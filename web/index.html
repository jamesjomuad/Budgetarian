Features

Everything you need to manage your money

Simple, powerful tools designed to help you understand your finances and build lasting habits.

📁

Budgets & Folders

Organize your finances into period-based budgets grouped by folders. Track spending across months, projects, or goals.

💰

Expense & Income Tracking

Record every transaction with amounts, dates, tags, and notes. Track paid and unpaid expenses with payment status.

📊

Transaction Breakdown

Split transactions into detailed breakdown items with their own dates, times, and amounts for granular tracking.

📅

Calendar & Reminders

View transactions in a monthly calendar and set reminders so you never miss a payment or financial obligation.

🏷️

Shared Tag Library

One global tag library used across every budget. Color-code and categorize transactions your way.

📈

Dashboard & Insights

See income, expenses, and remaining balances at a glance. Understand spending patterns with per-tag breakdowns.

📎

Receipts & OCR

Attach receipt photos to transactions. Scan receipt text automatically with on-device text recognition.

💾

Backup & Restore

Export your data as JSON for safekeeping. Import and merge backups. Export budgets to Excel, CSV, and more.

🔒

Private by Design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no server, no data collection. You stay in control.

Privacy

Your finances stay yours.

Budgetarian is designed to keep your financial information on your device. No account creation, no cloud sync, no data collection. You stay in control of your data.

📱

On-device storage. All data is stored locally on your phone using SQLite.

🚫

No account required. Start using Budgetarian immediately — no sign-up needed.

📤

Your backup, your choice. Export your data anytime as a JSON file for safekeeping.
